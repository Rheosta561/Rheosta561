# 💫 About Me:
Hi, I’m **Anubhav Mishra**, a startup founder and builder behind **PreacherClan**—a fitness ecosystem where ideas turn into real, living products. I love taking concepts straight from my mind to production, focusing on clean architecture, strong UX, and systems that scale from day one. I primarily build with **TypeScript-first stacks** like **React, Next.js, React Native, and Hono** to ship industry-grade web and mobile apps. **Agentic AI–enabled applications** are one of my favorite genres, especially when AI becomes an active part of workflows rather than just a feature. I believe in building ecosystems, not just apps.<br><br>If you want it **more aggressive**, **more minimal**, or **more AI-centric**, say the word.<br>
